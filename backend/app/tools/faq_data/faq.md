## What are your office hours?
We're open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to
1:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays and major holidays.

## What insurance do you accept?
We accept most major PPO insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna,
MetLife, and Aetna. We also offer an in-house savings plan for uninsured
patients. Call the office if you'd like us to verify a specific plan.

## What services do you offer?
We offer general cleanings and checkups, fillings, crowns, root canals,
teeth whitening, Invisalign, and emergency dental care.

## Where are you located?
123 Main Street, Springfield. We're in the shopping plaza next to the
pharmacy, with free parking in front of the building.

## Do you see new patients?
Yes, we welcome new patients. New patient visits include a full exam,
X-rays, and a cleaning, and typically take about 60-90 minutes.

## What should I do about a dental emergency?
If you're experiencing severe pain, swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, or a
knocked-out tooth, this is an emergency -- the agent should escalate
immediately to a human rather than attempting to schedule a routine slot.

## Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer in-house payment plans and financing through CareCredit for
larger treatment plans.
